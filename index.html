Why a pack

Running a business alone means being the marketer, the salesperson, the ops manager and the bookkeeper, usually with no time to do any of it well. A one-line prompt you found in a list somewhere needs rewriting before it is any use. Every prompt here is written long on purpose: it carries the context, the constraints and the place for your own details, so what comes back is about your business rather than generic advice.
